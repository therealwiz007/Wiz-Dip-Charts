Wiz Dip Charts turns a dip rod reading into tank stock in seconds. Type the dip in centimetres and read the volume in litres from the dip chart (calibration chart) for your underground tank. It is built for petrol pump dealers, managers, DSMs and supervisors who take dip readings every day for stock, DSR and tanker unloading.

What it does
• Dip to volume: enter the dip in cm (decimals allowed, like 87.4) and get litres from the chart.
• Volume to dip: enter litres and see the dip you should read. Useful before and after a tanker decants.
• 21 built-in dip charts for 10 KL, 15 KL, 20 KL, 50 KL and 70 KL horizontal tanks, including IS 2166 charts.
• Find my chart: not sure which chart is yours? Enter the litres your paper chart shows at 50 cm and 100 cm, and the app finds the matching one.
• My charts: keep only your own tanks on the main screen and name each one Petrol (MS), Diesel (HSD) or anything you like.
• Fill level and space left: see % full and how many litres of space are free before you order or unload a tanker.
• Today's stock: every tank you dipped today, with totals per product. Copy it or share it on WhatsApp as text.
• Tank details: diameter, length and deadwood are shown with each chart.
• Each tank remembers its last dip.
• Light and dark theme.

Works without internet
All charts are stored in the app. Your dip readings and stock stay on your phone. No account, no login.

Who uses it
Petrol pump (fuel station, retail outlet) dealers and staff, depot staff, fleet and industrial sites with underground diesel tanks, and auditors checking stock.

Accuracy note
The app reads values straight from the chart and interpolates between whole centimetres. Deadwood is shown as tank information and is not added or subtracted. Charts differ from tank to tank: always match the chart with the approved calibration chart for your tank before you use the numbers in official records.
