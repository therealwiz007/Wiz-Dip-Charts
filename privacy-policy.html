Privacy Policy

Wiz Dip Charts

Effective date: 30 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 30 October 2026

Wiz Labs (“we”, “us” or “our”) provides the Wiz Dip Charts mobile application (“App”). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Current version: The version of Wiz Dip Charts covered by this policy is designed to work offline. It does not currently include advertising SDKs or collect personal information through the App. We will update this policy before introducing advertising or any new data-collecting feature.

1. Information the App handles

The App uses built-in tank calibration chart data and the values you enter (such as tank selection, dip or volume) to perform calculations on your device. These values are processed locally to provide the App's functions.

We do not ask you to create an account, and the current version does not send your calculation inputs to our servers.

2. Information collected automatically

The current version does not contain an advertising SDK or analytics service, and it does not request internet access for its core calculator functions. We therefore do not currently collect analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, or payment information through the App.

Your device or the Google Play platform may independently process information under their own privacy terms. This policy does not cover data collected directly by Google Play or your device operating system.

3. Advertising and future changes

We may introduce advertising in a future version of the App. If we do, the advertising provider may process information such as an advertising ID, device or app information, approximate location, ad interactions, or diagnostic information, depending on the provider and your device settings.

Before enabling ads, we will update this Privacy Policy to identify the relevant advertising provider(s), explain the data they collect and use, and describe available choices. We will also update the Google Play Data safety declarations as required. Please review the policy again after App updates.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. In the current version, we do not transmit your calculation inputs to third parties. If advertising or other third-party services are added later, this section will be updated to explain the applicable sharing.

5. Data storage and retention

Calculation inputs are processed on your device. The current version does not maintain a user account or a remote user database. Any app preferences saved locally remain on your device until they are cleared or the App is uninstalled, subject to your device's Android settings.

6. Children's privacy

Wiz Dip Charts is a utility intended for people involved in fuel-storage measurement and related work. It is not designed specifically for children. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in a manner that requires action under applicable law, we will take appropriate steps.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the App and any information handled by it. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your choices

You can stop using the App and uninstall it at any time. You can clear locally stored App data through your Android device settings. If advertising is introduced, this policy will describe relevant advertising and privacy controls.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at the same privacy-policy URL, with the updated date shown at the top.
